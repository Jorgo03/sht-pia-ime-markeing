# Licensed music

Put licensed tracks here and reference them from a storyboard:
`"audio": {"music": {"src": "assets/music/<track>.m4a", "volume": 0.5}}`.

Without a track, films get an original bed composed to their own cuts (`"bed": "warm" | "night" | "bright" | "cinematic" | "paper"`), synthesized by `sound-design/music.mjs` — no licence needed. Never add copyrighted music you have not licensed for the platforms you publish on.
